# Storyboard

Create one section for every page, describing available evidence before polishing the scene.

## Page 1: Working title

### Beat

State the single story change that this page should add for readers.

### Start state

Describe what the reader sees before touching the page.

### Hand-driven change

Describe exactly what the reader pulls or lifts and what follows their hand.

### Result state

Describe the stable scene after the action finishes.

### Scene

Describe the setting, hero, supporting pieces, visual depth, and intended final pose.

### Pull tab

Describe the trial, tab movement, affected pieces, and important intermediate visual states.

### Lift flap

Describe the lesson, flap movement, revealed content, and intended calm final state.

### Facts

List every supporting fact ID and explain where readers should clearly see it.

### Open questions

List every unresolved question about evidence, wording, privacy, design, or accessibility.

### Owner sign-off

Record the owner's decision, reviewer, date, and every required follow-up change.
