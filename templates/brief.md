# Story brief

Complete this brief before research starts, keeping each answer clear enough to guide later decisions.

## Audience

Who should read this story, and what knowledge or context do they already have?

## Why now

Explain why this story should be made now rather than left until later.

## Voice

Describe the narrator, tone, tense, and intended relationship with each expected reader.

## Length

Set the total page count and target word range for every story page.

## Formats

List every required format, including browser, PDF, and printed copies for review.

## Privacy and exclusions

Name private details, topics, people, and projects that must remain outside this story.

## What changes from start to finish

State the starting situation, the key turning point, and the final state in three sentences.

## Reader takeaway

Write the one sentence a reader should remember after closing the book.

## Success test

Describe what readers should understand, feel, or remember after they finish the story.
