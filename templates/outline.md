# Story outline

Build the story arc from verified events, leaving uncertain events open until evidence arrives.

## Overall arc

### Beginning

Describe the starting situation and the first question that should engage each reader.

### Change

Describe the pressure, repeated attempts, meaningful setbacks, and most important turning point.

### Ending

Describe the final outcome, lasting meaning, and honest limits of the completed story.
